On your turn

  • Draw a tile, then discard one.
  • Chow: claim only from the player before you.
  • Pung or kong: claim from anyone.
  • A win beats a pung; a pung beats a chow.
  • A kong uses four tiles, so draw a replacement.
  • Flowers are set aside and replaced.

Claimed or concealed?
  • Most patterns still score with claimed sets.
  • Concealed Hand · 2: nothing claimed, won on a discard.
  • Fully Concealed Hand · 4: nothing claimed, self-drawn.
  • A pung finished by a discard is not a concealed pung.
